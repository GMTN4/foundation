About Us

Restoring Dignity • Building Hope • Creating Sustainable Communities

Foundation background

Gabisile Mbatha Thyala Ngesfuba Foundation NPC is a South African non-profit company established to uplift vulnerable communities through sustainable development, education, agriculture, youth empowerment, food security and social welfare.

We work in Gauteng and the Eastern Cape, with a geographical focus in KwaZulu-Natal. Our main address is in Sgwegwede Village, AbaQulusi Local Municipality, Ward 24, Coronation.

Our story

Our rallying call is “Kakade Sityhala Ngesifuba – Long live the Spirit of the Queen.” It reflects a simple belief: communities grow stronger when women, men and children are empowered together. Everything we do is guided by that belief.

Our vision

Communities where every woman, man and child lives with dignity, hope and opportunity.

Our mission

To empower women, men and children through sustainable programmes that restore dignity and build self-reliance.

Our purpose

To turn compassion into lasting community change through agriculture, education, youth and widow empowerment, and food security.

Our core values

  • Dignity
  • Hope
  • Empowerment
  • Sustainability
  • Community
  • Accountability

Strategic objectives

  • Strengthen household food security through gardens, farming and cooperatives.
  • Empower widows and women with skills, mentorship and small-business support.
  • Prepare young people for work through leadership, digital literacy and entrepreneurship.
  • Support learners with school resources, uniforms and mentorship.
  • Relieve poverty through community outreach and social relief.
  • Build lasting partnerships with businesses, government and donors.

Our commitment to communities

We are committed to transparency, financial accountability and the responsible management of donor funds, and to working alongside communities rather than for them.

Section 18A status

The foundation holds Section 18A approval under the Income Tax Act. Qualifying donations may be eligible for a Section 18A tax certificate. To request one, please contact us using the details above or the donation enquiry on the main website.

Merchandise purchases are sales of goods. They are not donations and do not qualify for Section 18A tax certificates.
