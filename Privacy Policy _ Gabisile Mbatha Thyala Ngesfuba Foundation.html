Privacy Policy

How we handle your personal information under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Draft for legal review. This policy describes how this website actually works today. It must be reviewed by a legal professional before it is relied on. Last updated: 9 November 2026.

Who we are

Gabisile Mbatha Thyala Ngesfuba Foundation NPC, Sgwegwede Village, AbaQulusi Local Municipality, Ward 24, Coronation 3100, KwaZulu-Natal. Telephone (079) 346-0979. An official privacy email address will be added once supplied.

What information we collect

Only what you type into our enquiry, donation and contact forms: your name, email address, phone number, whether you are an individual or organisation, the type of contribution, an optional estimated amount and your message. On the merchandise shop we also collect delivery details you enter when you request an order.

Why we collect it and how we use it

We use your information to respond to your enquiry, arrange a donation or partnership, and process merchandise order requests. We do not use it for anything else and we do not use it for marketing unless you ask us to.

How our forms work and where information is stored

Our forms prepare a message addressed to the foundation’s WhatsApp number. Your details leave this website only when you press send in WhatsApp. They are then held in the foundation’s WhatsApp account and are subject to WhatsApp’s own terms. This website does not run a database and does not store what you type.

How we protect your information

We collect only the information we need and keep it with the foundation’s representatives who respond to enquiries. Please avoid sending sensitive details such as identity numbers or bank details through our forms.

Sharing with third parties

We do not sell or rent your information. We share it only where the law requires. Fonts on this website are loaded from Google, which may receive your IP address when a page loads.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set cookies and does not run analytics at present. We will update this notice and ask for your consent if that changes.

Your rights

You may ask us to confirm what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Contact us on (079) 346-0979. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you may also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.

Questions about privacy?

Call us on (079) 346-0979 or use the contact form on our main website.
